# Demo Refund Policy

- Only damaged or incorrect items qualify for an automatic refund.
- Final-sale items are not refundable.
- Requests more than 30 days after purchase are denied.
- Eligible requests over $500 require human review.
- Suspicious, conflicting, or unclear requests require human review.
- Other reasons are denied.

If multiple rules apply, suspicious requests are escalated first. Then final-sale and age restrictions apply, followed by the $500 review threshold.
